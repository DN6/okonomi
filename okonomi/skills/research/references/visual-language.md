# Visual Language Reference

## Composition Frameworks

| Framework | Description | Use When |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Rule of thirds | Subject at intersection of 3x3 grid | General-purpose, natural balance |
| Golden ratio / spiral | Subject follows logarithmic spiral | Organic, flowing compositions |
| Centered symmetry | Subject dead center, symmetrical frame | Formal, powerful, confrontational |
| Leading lines | Lines draw eye to focal point | Guiding attention, depth |
| Frame within frame | Architectural/natural elements frame subject | Layers, voyeurism, containment |
| Negative space | Subject surrounded by emptiness | Isolation, minimalism, breathing room |
| Diagonal dominance | Strong diagonal lines create energy | Dynamic tension, movement |
| Triangular | Subject forms or sits within a triangle | Stability, hierarchy |
| Radial | Elements radiate from center point | Energy, explosion, mandala |
| Broken grid | Deliberate asymmetry, elements escaping structure | Tension, rebellion, modernity |

## Color Relationships

### Harmony Types
- **Monochromatic**: Single hue, varied value/saturation — unity, sophistication
- **Analogous**: Adjacent hues (e.g., blue-green-teal) — harmony, natural feel
- **Complementary**: Opposing hues (e.g., blue/orange) — vibrant contrast, energy
- **Split-complementary**: One hue + two adjacent to its complement — contrast with less tension
- **Triadic**: Three equally spaced hues — balanced vibrancy
- **Tetradic**: Four hues in complementary pairs — rich, complex

### Color Psychology (Western Defaults)
| Color | Associations | Caution |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Red | Passion, danger, energy, warmth | Culturally variable (luck in China, mourning in South Africa) |
| Blue | Trust, calm, depth, sadness | Can feel corporate if overused |
| Yellow | Joy, optimism, warning, intellect | Can feel cheap at high saturation |
| Green | Nature, growth, envy, health | Can skew toxic/artificial when shifted toward cyan |
| Purple | Royalty, mystery, spirituality | Can feel artificial |
| Orange | Energy, warmth, creativity, autumn | Often reads as casual/informal |
| Black | Elegance, power, death, void | Dominates easily |
| White | Purity, space, clinical, emptiness | Can feel sterile without texture |

### Temperature & Mood
- **Warm palette** (amber, gold, terracotta): Comfort, nostalgia, intimacy
- **Cool palette** (steel, teal, lavender): Distance, calm, technology
- **Mixed temperature**: Creates visual tension — warm subject on cool background draws the eye

## Lighting Vocabulary

### Quality
- **Hard light**: Sharp shadows, high contrast, dramatic (direct sun, spotlight)
- **Soft light**: Gradual shadows, low contrast, gentle (overcast, diffused)
- **Diffused**: Light scattered evenly, wrapping (window with curtain, softbox)

### Direction
- **Front**: Flat, even, reduces depth (passport photo, beauty lighting)
- **Side**: Reveals texture, creates drama (Rembrandt, split lighting)
- **Back**: Silhouette, rim glow, separation (golden hour, halo)
- **Top**: Harsh, dramatic, skull-like shadows (noon sun, interrogation)
- **Bottom**: Unnatural, eerie, horror (campfire, footlights)

### Named Setups
- **Rembrandt**: 45° from subject, triangle of light on shadow side cheek
- **Butterfly/Paramount**: Directly above camera, downward shadow under nose
- **Loop**: Slightly off-center from above, small nose shadow loops toward cheek
- **Split**: Light from 90° to one side, half the face lit
- **Broad**: Lit side of face faces camera (wider-looking face)
- **Short**: Shadow side of face faces camera (slimming, dramatic)

## Texture & Surface

### Texture Vocabulary
| Term | Visual Quality | Examples |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Rough | Irregular surface, catches light unevenly | Concrete, bark, burlap |
| Smooth | Even surface, clean reflections | Glass, polished metal, water |
| Organic | Irregular, natural patterns | Leaves, stone, skin |
| Geometric | Regular, manufactured patterns | Tiles, fabric weave, circuit boards |
| Translucent | Light passes through, scatters | Frosted glass, paper, petals |
| Reflective | Mirrors environment | Chrome, wet surfaces, eyes |
| Matte | Absorbs light, no sheen | Chalk, velvet, unfinished wood |
| Gritty | Fine particles, noise-like | Sand, film grain, rust |

## Art Movements Quick Reference

| Movement | Period | Visual Signature | Key Figures |
|----------|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Impressionism | 1860s-1880s | Visible brushwork, light/color over form | Monet, Renoir, Degas |
| Art Nouveau | 1890s-1910 | Organic curves, flowing lines, nature motifs | Mucha, Klimt, Gaudí |
| Expressionism | 1905-1920s | Distorted form, emotional color, psychological intensity | Munch, Kirchner, Schiele |
| Cubism | 1907-1920s | Fragmented geometry, multiple viewpoints | Picasso, Braque |
| Art Deco | 1920s-1930s | Geometric luxury, bold symmetry, metallic | Erté, Tamara de Lempicka |
| Surrealism | 1920s-1950s | Dream logic, impossible juxtapositions | Dalí, Magritte, Ernst |
| Abstract Expressionism | 1940s-1960s | Gestural, large-scale, raw emotion | Pollock, Rothko, de Kooning |
| Pop Art | 1950s-1970s | Mass culture, bold color, flat graphic | Warhol, Lichtenstein, Hockney |
| Minimalism | 1960s-1970s | Reduced form, geometric, essential | Judd, Flavin, Agnes Martin |
| Brutalism | 1950s-1970s | Raw concrete, monumental, unfinished | Corbusier, Tadao Ando |
| Vaporwave | 2010s | Retro-digital, pastel neon, glitch, Roman busts | Internet-born aesthetic |
| Solarpunk | 2010s-present | Green technology, organic architecture, utopian | Community-driven aesthetic |
