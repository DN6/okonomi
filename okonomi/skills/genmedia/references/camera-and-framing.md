# Camera Movements & Shot Framing

## Camera Movements

| Movement | Description | Example Prompt |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Static | Locked camera, no movement | "A static wide shot of a desert highway" |
| Pan (left/right) | Horizontal rotation on fixed point | "Camera pans left across a crowded marketplace" |
| Tilt (up/down) | Vertical rotation on fixed point | "Camera tilts up from muddy boots to a cowboy's face" |
| Dolly (in/out) | Camera physically moves toward/away from subject | "Camera dollies forward through a dark corridor" |
| Truck (left/right) | Camera moves laterally, parallel to subject | "Camera trucks right alongside a running athlete" |
| Pedestal (up/down) | Camera physically moves vertically, level perspective | "Pedestal up to reveal the full height of an ancient tree" |
| Crane/Boom | Camera rises or descends in sweeping arc | "Crane shot rising above a cityscape at sunset" |
| Tracking | Camera follows a subject in motion | "Camera tracks behind a cyclist weaving through traffic" |
| Orbit/Arc | Camera circles around subject | "Camera orbits slowly around a marble sculpture" |
| Zoom (in/out) | Lens changes focal length, camera stays put | "Slow zoom into the character's eyes" |
| Handheld/Shaky | Slight shake, documentary feel, immediacy | "Handheld close-up following a chef's hands" |
| Steadicam | Smooth floating movement through space | "Steadicam glides through a ballroom" |
| Whip pan | Extremely fast pan creating motion blur | "Whip pan from the speaker to the audience's reaction" |
| Aerial/Drone | High-altitude sweeping movement | "Aerial shot sweeping over coastal cliffs at golden hour" |

### Movement Combinations to Avoid
Stick to **one primary movement per clip**. Combining dolly + pan + zoom in a single generation leads to confused, unnatural motion.

## Shot Types (Framing)

| Shot | Description | Use For |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Extreme close-up | Isolates very small detail (eye, water drop) | Emotion, texture, revealing detail |
| Close-up | Tight on face or object | Emotion, reaction, intimacy |
| Medium close-up | Head and shoulders | Dialogue, character focus |
| Medium shot | Waist up | Conversation, balanced detail + context |
| Medium long shot | Knees up | Walking, gesture, partial environment |
| Full/Long shot | Head to toe with environment | Establishing character in space |
| Wide/Establishing shot | Broad environment, subject small | Setting location, scale, context |
| Extreme wide | Vast landscape, subject tiny or absent | Isolation, grandeur, world-building |

## Angle

| Angle | Effect | Example |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Eye-level | Neutral, natural | "Eye-level shot of a woman sipping tea" |
| Low-angle | Subject appears powerful, imposing | "Low-angle tracking shot of a superhero landing" |
| High-angle | Subject appears small, vulnerable | "High-angle shot of a child lost in a crowd" |
| Bird's-eye/Top-down | Map-like overhead view | "Bird's-eye view of a bustling intersection" |
| Worm's-eye | Very low, looking straight up | "Worm's-eye view of towering skyscrapers" |
| Dutch angle | Tilted horizon, conveys unease | "Dutch angle shot of a character running down a hallway" |
| Over-the-shoulder | Behind one person looking at another | "Over-the-shoulder shot during a tense negotiation" |
| POV | From character's direct perspective | "POV shot as someone rides a rollercoaster" |

## Lens & Optical Effects

| Lens/Effect | Description | Example |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Wide-angle | Broader field, exaggerated perspective | "Wide-angle lens of a grand cathedral interior" |
| Telephoto | Compressed perspective, isolates subject | "Telephoto capturing a distant eagle against mountains" |
| Shallow depth of field | Narrow focus plane, bokeh background | "Portrait with shallow depth of field, soft bokeh" |
| Deep depth of field | Everything sharp, foreground to background | "Landscape with sharp detail from wildflowers to mountains" |
| Lens flare | Streaks/starbursts from bright light source | "Cinematic lens flare as the sun dips below the horizon" |
| Rack focus | Focus shifts between subjects in shot | "Rack focus from a face in foreground to a photo on the wall" |
| Fisheye | Extreme barrel distortion, panoramic | "Fisheye view from inside a car, curved dashboard" |
| Dolly zoom (Vertigo) | Dolly + opposing zoom, background warps | "Vertigo effect on a character at the edge of a cliff" |
| Macro | Extreme close detail, shallow focus | "Macro lens on dewdrops clinging to a spider web" |
