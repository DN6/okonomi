### Cinematic Lighting Techniques
- **Rembrandt lighting**: Triangle of light on the shadowed cheek, classic portrait look
- **Chiaroscuro**: Stark contrast between light and dark, dramatic
- **High-key**: Bright, even, minimal shadows — cheerful, clean
- **Low-key**: Mostly dark with selective highlights — mysterious, moody
- **Rim/Back lighting**: Light from behind creating a glowing outline/silhouette
- **Volumetric lighting**: Visible light rays cutting through fog, dust, or smoke
- **Practical lighting**: Light sources visible in frame (lamps, candles, screens)

### Colored Light
- Cool blue: night, technology, isolation
- Warm amber: comfort, nostalgia, golden hour
- Red: danger, passion, urgency
- Green: nature, alien, sickness
- Neon mixed: cyberpunk, nightlife, urban energy
